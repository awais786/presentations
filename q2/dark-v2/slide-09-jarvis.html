Jarvis: Automating Routine Workflows
An internal tool that automates the routine filings every employee is required to complete, built by the team for the whole company
01
What It Automates
The required routine filings every member completes by hand today:
  • Log filling: the regular work logs every employee submits, drafted and filed automatically
  • Competency review filing: the annual review process, with competency data compiled and filed instead of assembled by hand
  • More required processes lined up as the plugin matures
02
Beta Tested, Feedback In
4-5 Beta Testers
Members ran Jarvis on their real daily workflows. Their feedback is driving the current improvement cycle, with fixes and refinements now landing for the next release.
03
Available Company-Wide
Ships as a Claude plugin. Install it, and the automation runs inside the AI tooling members already use daily. Available internally to everyone at Arbisoft today.
