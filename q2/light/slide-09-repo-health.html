Open edX Repo Health Dashboard

Live, public visibility into the health of the Open edX repository fleet · opensource.arbisoft.com
Live and public. Used by the Open edX community and core maintainers to see, at a glance, which repositories are healthy and which need attention. Delivered by Usama Sadiq.

What It Tracks

Health signals collected across the Open edX repo fleet and rendered in one view.

  • CI and build status
  • Dependency and version freshness
  • Maintenance ownership
  • Test and coverage signals

Who Uses It

Not an internal tool. It is community infrastructure - consumed by maintainers across the Open edX project when deciding where to spend effort.

Why It Matters

Owning a piece of shared community tooling puts Arbisoft in the room where platform-wide decisions get made - a standing that pure feature work does not buy.

Built on openedx/edx-repo-health - health checks run on a schedule, results published back to the community.
